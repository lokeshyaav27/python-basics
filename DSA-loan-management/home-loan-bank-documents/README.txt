Insurance is explicitly included in every bank's documents.

Property Insurance
- Provided: Yes
- Mandatory: No by default in demo configuration
- Insurance Amount: 0.10% of Loan Amount

Applicant Insurance
- Provided: Yes
- Mandatory: No by default in demo configuration
- Insurance Amount: 0.50% of Loan Amount

Example:
Loan Amount = Rs. 50,00,000
Property Insurance = 0.10% = Rs. 5,000
Applicant Insurance = 0.50% = Rs. 25,000

Formula:
Insurance Amount = Loan Amount x Insurance Percentage

Important:
These are synthetic/demo values for the DSA RAG project. They are not official bank
insurance premiums or official bank policy documents.
